Simple & Transparent

How GrammaConnect Works

Three simple steps to bridge community households with local skilled labor.

1

Post Work or Browse

Households describe the required job (electrical, plumbing, masonry) or browse local profiles directly.

2

Appoint Worker Directly

Send an appointment request or contact the worker via phone. No middlemen or commission charges.

3

Get Work Done & Rate

The worker completes the assignment, receives direct payment, and clients leave a rating to build community trust.

Community Service Project (CSP) Focus

Social Responsibility & Rural Digital Inclusion

GrammaConnect was conceptualized and developed as part of the Community Service Project curriculum. It addresses unemployment gaps and irregular wages among unorganized rural artisans by giving them free digital visibility in local Gram Panchayats.
